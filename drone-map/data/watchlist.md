## WINE TRADER — 15 October 2026, off Sulina, Romania

**Promoted on 16 October to [N090 — WINE TRADER](https://droneincidents.eu/events/2026-10-15-wine-trader-drone-strike-fire-sulina-romania/).**

Later reporting confirms a drone attack, with five crew rescued and seven missing.

TRADER and YARA J as Russian in origin. Its reported location, about 13 nautical
miles southeast of Sulina, is just outside territorial waters and falls within
the register’s offshore coverage. U066–U069 support the upgrade. The strike, fire
and rescue are one mapped event; this note does not add another record.

**Historical status at the 15 October 09:09 EEST check: follow-up only, not counted.**

about 14 nautical miles from Sulina. ARSVOM received the rescue request at 06:58;
SAR Apollo left at 07:10. A drone connection had not been established in that
report. A fire and rescue operation alone do not establish a drone strike.

The initial review kept this separate from YARA J (N086), whose reported incident
was the previous evening, pending findings about the cause. Later evidence led
to N090; casualties and outcomes remain specific to each vessel.

Source: [Euronews România, 15 October 08:16 live update](https://www.euronews.ro/articole/nava-lovita-de-o-drona-in-apele-teritoriale-ale-romaniei-langa-sulina-echipajul-a),
