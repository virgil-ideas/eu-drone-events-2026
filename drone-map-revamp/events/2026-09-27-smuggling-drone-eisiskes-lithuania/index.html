Eišiškės: border guards recover drone carrying cigarettes from Belarus

27 September 2026 · Eišiškės · cross-border smuggling drone

Officially reported cross-border smuggling-drone incident

VSAT reported a cigarette-carrying drone from Belarus before midnight, local time, on 27 September. A different patrol intercepted the drone near Eišiškės several hours after the first incident. The drone fell in the town area and was recovered.

Border guards recovered 100 packs of cigarettes and opened administrative proceedings. The operator was not identified. This is a cross-border smuggling case, not an established state attack.
