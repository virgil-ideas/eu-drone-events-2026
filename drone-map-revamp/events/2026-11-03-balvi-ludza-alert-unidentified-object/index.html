3 November 2026 · Balvi–Ludza · Air alert and preliminary object-fall report

Official alert; preliminary object fall, identity and recovery unresolved

Latvian armed forces issued an airspace warning for Balvi and Ludza shortly after midnight on 3 November. The official notices place the warning between 00:10 and 00:35 local.

An unidentified object reportedly fell in Balvi municipality, but the report is preliminary and ground searches continued. This remains a candidate, not a confirmed foreign-drone incursion. The map point represents the municipality, not a verified crash site.
