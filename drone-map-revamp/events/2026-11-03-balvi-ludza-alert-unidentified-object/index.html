Latvia confirms UAV detection; origin and possible Balvi fall unresolved

3 November 2026 · Balvi–Ludza · UAV detected; possible fall under investigation

UAV detection confirmed by NBS; origin and possible fall unresolved

NBS confirms a UAV was detected in Latvian airspace during the 3 November Balvi–Ludza alert. Its initial visual identification resembled Ukrainian military UAVs, but the armed forces explicitly could not confirm type or origin.

The UAV may have fallen in Balvi municipality; ground searches continued. This remains a candidate with unresolved origin and outcome, not a confirmed foreign incursion, crash or shoot-down. The regional map point is not a verified crash site.
