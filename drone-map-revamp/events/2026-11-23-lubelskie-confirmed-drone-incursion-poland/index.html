Russian-attributed drone enters Poland near Dorohusk and returns to Ukraine

23 November 2026 · Dorohusk · confirmed drone incursion

Polish Operational Command confirmed one brief drone entry and later exit

Poland’s Operational Command confirmed one Russian-attributed drone entered Polish airspace on 23 November during strikes on western Ukraine. Rzeczpospolita’s follow-up, citing the command and spokesperson Jacek Goryszewski, places entry near Dorohusk at 22:50 local time, with a roughly 20 km incursion lasting 8–9 minutes before the drone turned back to Ukraine.

A shoot-down was authorized, but no weapons were used. Radio Lublin and UNITED24 report that commanders withheld fire after the eastward turn, taking civilian safety into account. Goryszewski said ground systems had authorization; Polish aircraft were not operating near the border because of weather. No casualties, impact or debris recovery were reported.

The spokesperson assessed jet propulsion from flight speed but did not confirm cameras or a reconnaissance role. The model, payload and intended target remain unknown. The Dorohusk marker is an approximate reference, not a verified crossing coordinate. Public warnings are part of this one occurrence, distinct from the 20 November missile incident.
