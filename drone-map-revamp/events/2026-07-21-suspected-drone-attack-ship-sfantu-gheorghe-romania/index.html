GAS LISBON fire off Romania: one sailor died; drone cause unconfirmed

21 July 2026 · Off Sfântu Gheorghe · GAS LISBON

Suspected maritime attack; drone cause unconfirmed

The Liberia-flagged LPG tanker GAS LISBON suffered an explosion and fire off Sfântu Gheorghe in Romania’s EEZ, outside its territorial waters. The Naval Authority logged the distress alert at 00:05 local time on 21 July, not the verified explosion time.

Two crew members described a night-time attack by three drones. Their account is treated as an allegation, not a final identification of the weapon: the drone type, the devices and charges used, the launch point and the operator or nationality have not been identified, and a drone cause has not been confirmed.

This is separate from the 24 July bulk carrier emergency and the August PROGRESS IV casualty.

All 17 crew were evacuated. Three were injured; one of those three died on 23 July in Tulcea hospital, Romania’s Health Ministry told AGERPRES. Philippine government reporting corroborated the death and the return home of the other two injured seafarers. The casualty outcome does not establish a drone cause.
