Greece reports 8 Turkish UAVs and 12 airspace violations on 21 October

21 October 2026 · Northeastern / central / southeastern Aegean · Greek-reported Turkish UAVs

Greek-authority-reported Turkish UAV airspace violations

Greek authorities reported 8 Turkish UAVs in the northeastern / central / southeastern Aegean on 21 October 2026, with 12 national-airspace violations and 9 Athens FIR infringements. This is one dated record; the three counts measure different things.

Six accompanying F-16s and their five airspace violations and two FIR infringements are excluded. The UAVs were identified and intercepted; no armed engagement, casualties or damage was reported. The airspace assessment remains attributed to Greece. Militaire and NewsIT reproduce matching UAV-specific figures; To Vima corroborates the eight UAVs and regional activity but gives combined aircraft totals. The original GEETHA bulletin and precise tracks were not retrieved.
