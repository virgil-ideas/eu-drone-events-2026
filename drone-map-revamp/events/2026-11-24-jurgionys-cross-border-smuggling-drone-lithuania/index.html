Lithuanian guards recover a smuggling drone and 500 cigarette packs near Jurgionys

24 November 2026 · Jurgionys · cross-border smuggling drone

Officially reported cross-border smuggling-drone operation

Lithuanian border guards reported a drone entry from Belarus near Jurgionys before midnight on 24 November and recovered 500 packs of cigarettes.

The 26 November release says the aircraft was damaged on impact. It was the 74th aircraft intercepted in that enforcement category in 2026 as of the release, not the 74th record in this register.
