Drone-damaged MARIAM M drifts into Romanian waters and grounds at Vadu

16 November 2026 · MARIAM M · drifting vessel grounds at Vadu

Officially reported grounding of a previously drone-damaged vessel

MARIAM M grounded on Romania’s Vadu coast on 16 November 2026. The abandoned, Tanzania-flagged cargo vessel had drifted into Romanian waters after its anchor broke on 14 November.

Romanian authorities say a September drone strike in Ukrainian territorial waters had left the ship anchored there. This record covers its subsequent arrival and grounding in Romania, classified as Alert / other. It does not count a new drone strike or transfer casualties from the September attack.

On 19 November tugs freed the vessel from its original sandbank. ProTV later reported that it moved about 100 metres before grounding on another sandbar, after which the crew anchored it and paused salvage around nightfall. Final berth and environmental outcome are not established. The page includes earlier ARSVOM response footage and a shoreline video link.

What was reported
ANR says a September drone strike in Ukrainian territorial waters left the vessel anchored. Its anchor broke on 14 November. Currents carried it into Romanian waters, where MRCC monitored it. It grounded at Vadu on 16 November before towing could begin.
Aircraft / vessel / quantity
MARIAM M, Tanzania-flagged cargo vessel, reported abandoned without crew or cargo aboard.

Damage / casualties / disruption
Vessel grounded and became a navigation hazard. No new casualties reported in Romania; September attack casualties are not counted here.
Response / outcome
Navigation warning issued. SAR ARTEMIS found the ship already grounded on 16 November. The owner contracted COREMAR for refloating. On 19 November, ProTV observed the ship begin moving during the recovery operation; Replica Online subsequently reported that two tugs pulled it clear of the grounding area. Later on 19 November, ProTV reported that it moved about 100 metres before grounding again on another sandbar. The ship agent said the stern remained on sand. The vessel was anchored and salvage paused around nightfall.
