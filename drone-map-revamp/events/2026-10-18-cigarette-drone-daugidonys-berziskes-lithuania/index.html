Crash · Lithuania

Daugidonys–Beržiškės · cross-border cigarette drone intercepted in Lithuania

18 October 2026 · Daugidonys–Beržiškės · cross-border cigarette drone

VSAT-confirmed cross-border cargo drone intercepted and recovered

Sunday night, 18 October 2026; midnight boundary unresolved: Surveillance recorded the aircraft falling after electronic intervention. Guards recovered the damaged drone and detached cargo minutes later. 750 Minsk 5 Superslims cigarette packs with Belarusian tax stamps.

Detected near Daugidonys; recovered in woodland near Beržiškės, Šalčininkai district, about 1,050 m from the Belarus border. Cross-border criminal transport from Belarus according to VSAT; operator nationality and state sponsorship unestablished.

VSAT's 19 October report says 'sekmadienio naktį' (Sunday night), without a clock time. Replay uses the named Sunday, 18 October, as an inferred date; the source does not resolve whether the overnight interval began on 17 October or continued into 19 October. No precise midnight boundary is asserted. Surveillance-observed fall and impact damage support crash; electronic intervention is not a kinetic shoot-down.
