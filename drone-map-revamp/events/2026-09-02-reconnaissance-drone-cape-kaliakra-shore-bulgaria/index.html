Reconnaissance drone found on Cape Kaliakra seashore in Bulgaria

2 September 2026 · Cape Kaliakra — shore

Reconnaissance UAV officially identified; origin unresolved

On 2 September 2026 naval personnel identified an object found on the seashore at Cape Kaliakra, on Bulgaria's Black Sea coast, as a reconnaissance drone (UAV). The find was announced on 3 September.

The drone's model, operator and manufacturer have not been announced, and its origin is unresolved. When it flew or came down, and whether it is linked to other drones recovered along the coast, are not known. No payload was reported.

The drone was transported to Varna Naval Base. This shoreline recovery is separate from an offshore operation on 6 September.
