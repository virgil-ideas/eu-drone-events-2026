Crash · Romania

Drone enters Romanian airspace and crashes near Parcheș

26 March 2026 · Parcheș · 26 March incursion and crash

Officially confirmed drone incursion and crash

MApN reported a drone entering at 00:44 on 26 March, penetrating about 4 km and crashing outside Parcheș. Teams found debris and burned vegetation; no material damage or casualties were reported.

The ministry said Ukrainian air defence diverted the drone. This account remains attributed to MApN; the release does not establish its operator, model or an intention to attack Romania.
