Greece reports Turkish UAV overflight of Zourafa islet in the Aegean

18 September 2026 · Zourafa — islet overflight

Greek-reported Turkish UAV overflight of a named islet

On 18 September 2026 a UAV flew over Zourafa, an islet in the northeastern Aegean, according to a Greek military account relayed the same day by the Greek outlet Politica. The exact time and the full route were not published, and the drone’s model was not specified.

The Greek account identified the drone as Turkish and presented the overflight as an airspace violation; the track has not been independently verified. The same account reported a formation of manned F-16s over Ro, which is not a drone incident. For the whole day it listed five UAVs among ten aircraft, with 13 flight information region (FIR) infringements and seven airspace violations by mixed aircraft; how many of these involved drones was not specified.

No impact or casualties were reported, and the account did not describe a specific response to the Zourafa overflight.
