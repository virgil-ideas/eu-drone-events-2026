Greece reports six Turkish UAVs and thirteen airspace violations on 23 October

23 October 2026 · Northeastern / central / southeastern Aegean · Greek-reported Turkish UAVs

Greek-authority-reported Turkish UAV airspace violations

The Greek military account reproduced by OnAlert and NewsIT lists six Turkish UAVs, thirteen national-airspace violations and nine Athens FIR infringements in the northeastern, central and southeastern Aegean on 23 October 2026. These are distinct measures in one daily-activity record.

A CN-235 and its two FIR infringements are excluded. Identification and interception were reported, but no armed UAV engagement is established. Flight tracks, times and payloads remain unknown; the airspace assessment is attributed to Greece, and the original military bulletin was not retrieved directly.
