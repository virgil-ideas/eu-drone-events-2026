Two drone fragments without explosives found at Câșla Vădanei, Romania

11 September 2026 · Câșla Vădanei — beach

Official aerial-drone fragment assessment; origin unknown

On 11 September 2026 two aerial-drone fragments were found washed ashore on the beach at Câșla Vădanei, between Sulina and Sfântu Gheorghe in Tulcea, Romania. An explosive ordnance disposal (EOD) team from the Defence Ministry (MApN) assessed them at 13:30, which is the response time rather than a known crash time, and found no explosive charge.

The Ministry's account gives no operator or national attribution, and the origin of the fragments is unknown. It is not known how many aircraft they came from, and the flight or crash date is unresolved.
