Aegean · one UAV in Greek daily report for 18 November

18 November 2026 · Aegean · 18 November reported Turkish UAV activity

Reported Greek-authority daily figures for Turkish UAV airspace violations

GEETHA figures reproduced by OnAlert and NewsIT attribute one national-airspace violation and one separate Athens FIR infringement to a Turkish UAV on 18 November in the northeastern and central Aegean.

The manned CN-235 in the same table is excluded from UAV counts. Routine interception is reported, with no engagements. These outlets reproduce the same authority account, not independent tracks; the precise route, model and intent remain unknown.
