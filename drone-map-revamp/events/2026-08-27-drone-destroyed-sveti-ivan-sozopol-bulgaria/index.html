Navy destroys drone spotted near Sveti Ivan island at Sozopol, Bulgaria

27 August 2026 · Sozopol · Sveti Ivan island

Coastal aerial-drone recovery; individual origin unresolved

On 27 August 2026 a drone (UAV) was spotted at the northern pier opposite Sveti Ivan island, in the Sozopol area on Bulgaria's Black Sea coast. The Bulgarian Navy destroyed the object in place, in a controlled disposal announced on 28 August; the exact time of the disposal was not specified.

The origin of this drone has not been established, and no operator has been identified. Broader Russian or Ukrainian associations made for this group of coastal finds do not identify this particular object. When it came down, and the route it flew, are unknown.

The exact hazard posed by the object was not specified, and no casualties were reported. The destruction was a controlled disposal by the authorities, not an explosion caused by the drone itself.
