Suspected drone fragment found by Constanța Casino promenade, Romania

25 August 2026 · Constanța Casino promenade

Suspected drone fragment; origin unresolved

On the evening of 25 August 2026 passers-by noticed an object near the Constanța Casino in Romania. The object, a suspected drone component, had reportedly washed ashore between the breakwaters by the casino promenade. The find was reported in coverage on 26 August.

The object has not been confirmed as part of a drone, and its full identity, origin and operator have not been established. No explosives were identified when it was examined.

Firefighters and specialists recovered and examined the object, and no harm was reported. It is a separate find from the explosive drone found on Olimp beach the next morning.
