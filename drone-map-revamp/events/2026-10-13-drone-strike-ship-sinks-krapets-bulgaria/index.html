Strike · Bulgaria

HS Dignity identified as ship sunk off Krapets; eleven sailors missing

13 October 2026 · HS Dignity off Krapets · Drone strike and sinking

Reported drone attack and sinking; HS Dignity identified; operator unresolved

The merchant ship reported struck by a drone and sunk off Krapets on 13 October 2026 has been identified as HS Dignity, sailing under the Guinea-Bissau flag. The reported location, about 17 nautical miles east of Krapets, is in Bulgaria’s exclusive economic zone, outside territorial waters. The exact strike and sinking times remain unpublished.

Later reporting gives five sailors rescued and eleven missing, replacing the initial account of one rescue. Search-and-rescue operations continued at the time of that reporting. The missing sailors are not counted as confirmed dead. The identification and rescue update concern the same sinking, not another attack.

The drone’s type and operator remain unresolved. Ukraine accused Russia, but Bulgarian authorities have not established responsibility conclusively. The vessel’s flag does not identify its crew’s nationalities. This incident remains separate from the CHELSEA 3 attack near Byala and the 6 October attacks on ALFA WATAN and ABLE.
