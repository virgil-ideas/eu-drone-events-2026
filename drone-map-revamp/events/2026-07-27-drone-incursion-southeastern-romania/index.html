Drone reported as Russian briefly enters southeastern Romania

27 July 2026 · Southeastern Romania

Reported foreign; limited detail

On 27 July 2026 a drone (UAV) was reported to have made a brief incursion into Romania's southeastern airspace before exiting into Ukraine. Its exact route was not specified. Romanian air defences monitored and responded to the flight, but the drone departed before it could be engaged.

The drone was reported as Russian, but details of the incident are limited: no technical findings were available, and the drone's model and payload were not reported. No impact was reported in Romania.
