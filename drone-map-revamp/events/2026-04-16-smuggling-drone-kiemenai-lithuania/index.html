Kiemėnai: Lithuanian border guards recover smuggling drone

16 April 2026 – 17 April 2026 · Kiemėnai · cross-border smuggling drone

Officially reported cross-border smuggling-drone operation

16–17 April 2026. VSAT said it detected the drone flying into Lithuania from Belarus. The drone fell after border guards intervened and was recovered near Kiemėnai.

Officers recovered 750 packs of Belarus-stamped cigarettes and opened administrative proceedings.

No operator, state sponsorship or exact flight track was established. This is a border-smuggling record, not a military attack.
