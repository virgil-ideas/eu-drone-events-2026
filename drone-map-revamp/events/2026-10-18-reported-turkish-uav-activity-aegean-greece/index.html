Aegean: 6 reported airspace violations on 18 October

18 October 2026 · Aegean · 18 October reported Turkish UAV activity

Single-source report of Greek-authority figures for Turkish UAV airspace violations

Veteranos, citing Greek military reporting, described 5 Turkish UAVs in the central and southeastern Aegean on 18 October 2026, with 6 reported national-airspace violations and, separately, 10 Athens FIR traffic-rule infringements.

The account reports identification and routine interception, not a weapons engagement or a shoot-down.

This is one attributed daily record, not a count of separate confirmed incursions. The original Greek military register entry was not obtained, and tracks, exact locations, intent and operator details are unverified. The Greek–Turkish airspace dispute remains relevant.
