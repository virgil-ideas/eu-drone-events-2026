Drone attack off Bulgaria sinks ALFA WATAN and sets ABLE on fire

6 October 2026 · ALFA WATAN / ABLE · offshore drone attack

ALFA WATAN sank, crew missing; ABLE crew rescued, vessel still afloat in 9 October report

In the early hours of 6 October 2026 two merchant ships, the Togo-flagged ALFA WATAN and the Palau-flagged ABLE, were attacked about 70 nautical miles east of Byala, in Bulgaria’s exclusive economic zone outside its territorial waters. Bulgarian authorities reported aerial and maritime attack drones. ALFA WATAN sank, and the nearby ferry DIOSCURIA witnessed an explosion; ABLE caught fire.

Bulgaria has not attributed the attack. Rumen Radev said on 7 October that the circumstances suggested Russia but the evidence was insufficient for definitive attribution; Russia denied targeting the vessel. On 6 October Zelensky alleged a combined Russian attack, citing a joint investigation; that is a Ukrainian claim, not a Bulgarian finding. On 9 October Defence Minister Dimitar Stoyanov assessed ABLE’s damage as a surface-drone strike, without naming an operator.

ALFA WATAN’s crew are missing, not confirmed dead. Reuters reported twelve Syrians missing on 7 October, then ten missing, including nine Syrians, on 8 October; the discrepancy is unresolved. All eighteen ABLE crew were rescued, two of them injured. Bulgaria halted the search on 7 October, and on 8 October Syria sought an independent investigation. A 9 October inspection found no identifiable drone remnants aboard ABLE, which was still afloat.

Response / outcome

On 8 October Syria sought an independent investigation into the attack and termination of the search. A Cougar helicopter and frigate Verni inspected ABLE; two personnel boarded but found no identifiable drone remnants (9 October reporting).
