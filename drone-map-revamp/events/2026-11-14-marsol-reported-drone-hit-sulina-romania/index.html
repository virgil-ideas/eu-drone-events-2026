MARSOL hit by drone off Sulina, according to reported ministry account

14 November 2026 · MARSOL · reported drone hit off Sulina

Drone hit reported by MApN via News.ro, relaying civilian maritime authorities

MARSOL was hit by a drone at about 21:45 local on 14 November 2026, according to News.ro’s account of MApN information from civilian maritime authorities. The reported location was approximately 5 nautical miles southeast of the Sulina Canal.

The crew extinguished an onboard fire; no casualties were reported. Radio România Constanța describes Romanian territorial waters, but exact impact coordinates and jurisdiction have not been independently verified.

Operator, origin, intent and any connection to other vessel hits or nearby alerts remain unknown. An earlier maritime report gives 21:29 CET instead; that timing discrepancy remains unresolved.
