Lithuanian guards report a drone entry near Purvėnai and recovery near Geismantai

24 January 2026 · Purvėnai–Geismantai · cross-border smuggling drone

Officially reported cross-border smuggling-drone operation

Lithuania’s border service reported a drone entry on 24 January and recovered it near Geismantai with 1,000 packs of cigarettes.

This is distinct from the separately reported 27 January Purvėnai incident.
