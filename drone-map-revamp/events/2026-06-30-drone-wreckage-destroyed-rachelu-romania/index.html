Romania destroys drone fragments near Rachelu it identified as Russian

30 June 2026 · Rachelu

Foreign-drone debris disposal

On 30 June 2026 the Romanian military carried out a controlled detonation of drone fragments near Rachelu, in Tulcea County, Romania. The recovered material posed an explosive hazard, of an unspecified size, and required explosive-ordnance disposal. 30 June is the disposal date: when the drone flew, and when the fragments were found, has not been established.

Romania's Ministry of Defence identified the fragments as belonging to a Russian drone. The drone's original route is unspecified and the individual airframe has not been identified.

No casualties were reported. The disposal is treated as a recovery of wreckage, not as a separately demonstrated new incursion into Romania.
