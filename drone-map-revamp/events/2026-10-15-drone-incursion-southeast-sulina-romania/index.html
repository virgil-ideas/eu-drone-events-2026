Drone briefly enters Romanian airspace southeast of Sulina at 01:34

15 October 2026 · Southeast of Sulina · 01:34 drone incursion

Officially confirmed drone airspace incursion; operator unresolved

A drone was detected in Romanian airspace over territorial waters southeast of Sulina at 01:34 on 15 October 2026. The Defence Ministry said it remained visible for about one minute before disappearing from radar.

Spanish F-18s launched during the overnight alert. Romanian F-16s were later deployed for other contacts farther east of Snake Island, followed by a 04:57 warning. The release reported no further entries and an all-clear at 05:46.

This is a separate post-midnight incursion. The drone’s model and operator are unknown. Loss of radar contact does not establish a shoot-down, crash or impact; none was confirmed in the reviewed statement.
