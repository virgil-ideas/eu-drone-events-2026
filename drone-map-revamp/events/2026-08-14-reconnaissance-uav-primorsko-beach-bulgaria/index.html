Recovery · Bulgaria

Reconnaissance drone of unknown origin washes ashore at Primorsko, Bulgaria

14 August 2026 · Primorsko beach

Coastal aerial-drone recovery; individual origin unresolved

On 14 August 2026 a reconnaissance drone (UAV) washed ashore on the beach at Primorsko, in Bulgaria’s Burgas district. It was examined, and the Bulgarian Navy transported it to a naval base.

No operator has been identified for the drone, and the release reporting the find gave no final identification. Associations with Russian or Ukrainian drones made for the wider group of finds do not identify this one. When it came down and what route it flew are unknown.

No casualties were reported, and any payload was not specified.
