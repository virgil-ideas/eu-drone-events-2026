Wreckage of suspected Ukrainian drone found near Utena, Lithuania

17 May 2026 · Samanė · Utena area

Suspected foreign recovery

On 17 May 2026 wreckage of a suspected military drone was found in the Samanė and Utena area of Lithuania, and explosive material was then found nearby, with follow-up reports on 18 May. The date is that of the discovery: the drone’s flight path is unknown, and it has not been established that it crashed or exploded on that day.

The drone was suspected to be Ukrainian, but this was not stated as a final forensic identification, and its technical identification was reported to be under investigation. The explosives are linked to the same wreckage and are not taken to indicate a second drone.

No injuries were reported. Authorities cordoned off the site, recovered the material and investigated.
