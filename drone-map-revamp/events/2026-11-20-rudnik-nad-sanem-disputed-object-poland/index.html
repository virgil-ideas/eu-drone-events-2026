Unidentified object · Recovery · Poland

Object fell near Rudnik nad Sanem; drone and missile assessments differ

20 November 2026 · Rudnik nad Sanem · disputed object identification after Polish airspace breach

Confirmed airspace breach and fall; authorities differ on object type

Poland confirmed three objects entered its airspace on 20 November, one of which fell near Rudnik nad Sanem. The National Security Bureau initially assessed this object as a probable missile. The Podkarpackie Provincial Office reported the fall at 06:38 local time.

Later that day, Podkarpackie governor Teresa Kubas-Hul described it as an unmanned aircraft in remarks reported by RMF24. Investigators had not conclusively established the type at review. This is a separate provisional case within the same wider breach as Mołodycz, not a confirmed drone attack.

The Military Gendarmerie said initial specialist checks had ruled out radiological, chemical and explosive hazards at the recovery sites. On 21 November prosecutors reported that they had recovered and secured all located traces and object fragments at Rudnik. That did not establish the object type.
