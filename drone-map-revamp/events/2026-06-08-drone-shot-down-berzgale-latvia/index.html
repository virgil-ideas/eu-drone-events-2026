Shot down · Latvia

French NATO fighter shoots down drone near Bērzgale, Latvia

8 June 2026 · Bērzgale

Foreign-drone shoot-down; origin unresolved

On 8 June 2026 a drone (UAV) entered Latvian airspace and was intercepted in the Bērzgale area of Latvia. A French fighter jet operating with NATO then shot it down.

The drone's nationality and model were not conclusively identified in contemporaneous accounts of the incident. It is treated as a foreign drone, but its origin has not been established; its route was not specified, and what, if anything, it carried remains unknown.

No casualties were reported in Latvia. After the shoot-down, a search for the wreckage and an investigation of the debris followed.
