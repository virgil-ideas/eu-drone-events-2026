Tulcea residents warned overnight; Romania reported no airspace entry

22 November 2026 · Northern and eastern Tulcea · overnight RO-Alert

Selected RO-Alert; no entry into Romanian airspace reported

An overnight warning went to northern and eastern Tulcea at 00:46 local time on 22 November. Romania’s Defence Ministry reported one aerial target outside national airspace and said two F-16s took off. It reported no national-airspace entry. The ministry said the air alert ended at 02:14, while ISU Delta Tulcea gave 02:16 and reported no requests or problems. These are accounts of one warning episode.
