Flight · Latvia

Latvia assesses November 1 crossing UAV as likely a Russian Geran

1 November 2026 · Krāslava–Ludza · Foreign drone crosses Latvian airspace

Officially described drone incursion; exited into Russia

Officials described a drone crossing eastern Latvia from Belarus and leaving for Russia at 19:16 on 1 November. NBS subsequently assessed it as likely a Russian Geran after analysing sensor and interceptor data and consulting Ukrainian partners.

Latvia self-destructed its own interceptor at 19:19. This was not a reported shoot-down of the detected UAV. NBS cannot establish whether that UAV carried explosives; operator, intent and target remain unconfirmed.
