Sea Destiny reports nearby explosion after NATO names it as targeted ship

14 October 2026 – 15 October 2026 · Sea Destiny near Sulina · Suspected attempted drone attack

NATO-reported targeting; nearby explosion reported by the ship, with no damage; drone causation unresolved

NATO named the Marshall Islands-flagged bulk carrier Sea Destiny as an additional targeted ship during the 14–15 October incidents near Sulina. Romania’s defence minister said it reported an explosion within visual range but was unaffected. The vessel was reported inside Romanian territorial waters.

The incident remains provisional. A drone-caused explosion, a hit on the ship and its operator have not been confirmed. The Russian-origin assessment reported for the drones that struck YARA J and WINE TRADER is not specifically an attribution for Sea Destiny. No damage, casualties, recovery or EOD operation was reported.

The exact explosion time and location remain unpublished. Replay uses 14 October, the first day of the overnight range. This record remains separate from the two tankers; Dania J at Izmail, Ukraine, is outside the register’s EU scope.
