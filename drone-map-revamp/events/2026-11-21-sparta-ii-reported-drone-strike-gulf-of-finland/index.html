SPARTA II crew evacuated after reported drone strike in the Gulf of Finland

21 November 2026 · SPARTA II · reported drone strike and Finnish maritime rescue

Reported offshore drone strike; crew evacuated; firefighting ongoing at last primary update

Finland’s Defence Forces reported a drone strike on 21 November in the eastern Gulf of Finland. The military placed it in international waters, while Border Guard commander Ilja Iljin said it happened in Russian territorial waters. The burning ship later drifted into Finnish territorial waters. That does not establish a drone crossing into Finland.

The Finnish Border Guard confirmed that the crew left by lifeboat and transferred to another cargo vessel. Iljin reported no injuries. Firefighting continued at the Guard’s 18:55 UTC update; a tow was planned. Final fire, tow and pollution outcomes were not established in that update.

Finnish Defence Forces communications director Aki-Mauri Huhtinen assessed Ukraine was likely responsible. The specific operator, intent and cargo were not independently established.

Estonia’s Foreign Ministry says the damaged vessel drifted from Estonia’s exclusive economic zone into Finland’s exclusive economic zone, then into Finnish territorial waters. Estonia assisted at Finland’s request; the Finnish Border Guard confirmed Estonian participation in firefighting and cooling. The rescue accounts do not establish the strike location or route of a drone.
