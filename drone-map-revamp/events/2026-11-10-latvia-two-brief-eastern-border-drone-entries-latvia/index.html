Latvia · Two brief eastern-border drone entries

10 November 2026 · Latvia · Two brief eastern-border drone entries

NBS-confirmed brief drone incursions

Latvian NBS said two drones briefly crossed the eastern border for 1–2 minutes. It places the associated Ludza warning at approximately 23:30 on Tuesday 10 November.

Drone-interceptor and air-defence units and NATO fighters activated.

Exact crossing points, operator, payload and intent unresolved. Later Krāslava/Augšdaugava and Balvi approaches did not enter; not extra incursions.
