Romania shoots down drone it identified as Russian off Sulina

26 July 2026 · Northeast of Sulina

Reported foreign

On 26 July 2026 a drone (UAV) approaching from the Black Sea was shot down by a Romanian F-16 over Romanian territorial waters, about 12 km northeast of Sulina, Romania. It was the third drone Romania had destroyed on three successive days.

Romania identified it as a Russian drone. Its model was not specified, and the investigation into its model and payload had not been completed when the incident was reported.

No casualties were reported. An ambassador was summoned over the repeated violations.
