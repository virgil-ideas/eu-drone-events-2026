Crash · Lithuania

Smuggling drone recovered without cargo near Dumblė

23 May 2026 · Šalčininkai border district

Cross-border smuggling

On 23 May 2026 a homemade drone reported by Lithuania’s border guard service (VSAT) to have entered from Belarus fell near Dumblė, in Šalčininkai district, after border guards intervened.

When officers recovered the aircraft, it was badly damaged and had no cargo. The original VSAT notice corrects the earlier description of a loaded drone.

VSAT described the aircraft as belonging to smugglers. The operator is not known. No casualties or kinetic shoot-down were reported.
