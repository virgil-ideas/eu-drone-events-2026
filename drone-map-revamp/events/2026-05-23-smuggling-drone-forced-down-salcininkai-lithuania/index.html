Flight · Lithuania

Homemade smuggling drone forced down near Belarus border in Lithuania

23 May 2026 · Šalčininkai border district

Cross-border smuggling

On 23 May 2026 a homemade cargo drone carrying smuggled goods was reportedly forced down at Dumblė, in Lithuania's Šalčininkai district on the border with Belarus. Border enforcement officers intercepted the drone and seized it.

The drone was reported to have arrived from Belarus. It was a criminal smuggling flight, not a military one, and the operator has not been identified. Details of the cargo and of the drone itself were not available.

No explosive payload and no casualties were reported. Reports describe a forced landing and seizure, not a shoot-down or a crash.
