Farmakonisi · Turkish UAV overflight reported on 30 July

30 July 2026 · Farmakonisi · reported Turkish UAV overflight

Greek-authority account of Turkish UAV overflight

Greek reports attributed eight national-airspace violations to two Turkish UAVs on 30 July 2026, including an overflight of Farmakonisi. Four separate Athens FIR infringements were attributed to the UAVs. This is one daily record, not twelve incidents.

A ministerial answer dated 1 November and published 16 November acknowledges the overflight and says Greece sent an official report to NATO. It said NATO analysis was expected at that time; it provides neither a NATO finding nor evidence that a response is still pending. Exact flight tracks and intent remain unknown.
