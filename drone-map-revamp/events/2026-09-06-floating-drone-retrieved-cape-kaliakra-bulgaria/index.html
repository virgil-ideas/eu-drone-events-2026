Navy retrieves drone found afloat off Cape Kaliakra in Bulgaria

6 September 2026 · Cape Kaliakra — northeast coastal waters

Official retrieval reported; operator unresolved

In the late afternoon of 6 September 2026 a Navy team from Varna retrieved a floating drone (UAV) from coastal waters about 3.5 cables, or 650 m, northeast of Cape Kaliakra on Bulgaria's Black Sea coast. The team was activated at the request of the Interior Ministry. The operation was reported on 7 September.

The drone's type, origin and operator have not been announced. When it flew or came down, and whether it is linked to other drones recovered along the coast, are unresolved.

The drone was assessed as safe for retrieval, although no detailed payload information was published, and it was taken to the naval base.
