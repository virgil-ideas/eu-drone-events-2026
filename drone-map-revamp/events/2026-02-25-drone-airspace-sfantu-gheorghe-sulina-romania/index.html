Drone reported as Russian enters Romanian airspace near Sfântu Gheorghe

25 February 2026 · Sfântu Gheorghe · Sulina

Reported foreign

On 25 February 2026 radar detected a drone entering Romanian airspace over territorial waters near Sfântu Gheorghe, in Tulcea, during attacks on Ukrainian ports on the Danube. The drone left it north of Sulina. The incursion was confirmed as short; one earlier report put it at about 20 minutes, but that figure is unverified and the exact duration has not been established.

The drone was reported as Russian, and Romania’s defence ministry (MApN) connected the incident to Russian strikes on Ukraine. Its model is unknown.

Two F-16 fighter jets were scrambled at 17:50 local time, and an RO-Alert warning was issued at 18:07. The alert ended at about 18:45. No harm or debris was reported.
