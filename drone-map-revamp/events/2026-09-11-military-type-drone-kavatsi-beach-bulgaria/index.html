Tourists find damaged military-type drone on Kavatsi beach in Bulgaria

11 September 2026 · Kavatsi — beach

Military-type drone find reported; attribution and final assessment unresolved

On 11 September 2026 tourists found a drone washed up on the Kavatsi tourist beach near Sozopol, on Bulgaria's Black Sea coast. The drone body was damaged and its model was not specified. Specialists were summoned and the area was secured.

Reports described the drone as military in appearance, but that is not a confirmed national or operator attribution, and no final assessment has been reported. When it flew or came down, and whether it is linked to other drones recovered along the coast, are unresolved.

The final disposal outcome was not established in the report available. The Kavatsi find is separate from a late-August find at the Sveti Ivan pier in the Sozopol area.
