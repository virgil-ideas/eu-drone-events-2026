Estonia issued and lifted an air-threat warning in two northeastern counties

21 November 2026 · Northeastern Estonia · possible air-threat warning

Selected public air-threat warning; no verified crossing

Estonia’s Defence Forces issued a possible air-threat warning for Lääne-Viru and Ida-Viru on 21 November at 04:42 local time. The message advised taking shelter if a drone was seen. An all-clear followed at 06:49 local time. This is one warning episode, not a verified incursion.
