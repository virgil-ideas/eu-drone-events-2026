Unarmed foam aircraft found in Lubaczów; origin unresolved

21 October 2026 · Lubaczów · Unarmed foam-airframe discovery

Unarmed foam airframe confirmed by military police; origin unresolved

Wreckage was reported on an unoccupied Herzoga Street property in Lubaczów shortly after 13:00 on 21 October 2026. Nearby residents were evacuated while specialists assessed possible hazards; no injuries or property damage were reported.

Military police identified an unarmed foam airframe and excluded explosives and radiological, biological or chemical hazards on 22 October. The prosecutor’s 23 October update reports transfer of the drone to military counterintelligence and testing of its attached package in Warsaw. The preliminary short-range assessment does not establish origin or foreign entry. This remains one provisional recovery candidate.
