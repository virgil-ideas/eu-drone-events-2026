Drone fragments of unknown origin found on Costinești beach, Romania

13 August 2026 · Costinești beach

Linked coastal discoveries; airframe identity unresolved

At around 08:15 on 13 August 2026, aerial-drone material was reported near the breakwaters of Tineretului beach in Costinești, Romania, after drifting ashore. Tourist access was restricted. Another fragment was found nearby the next day, 14 August.

The origin and operator of the material have not been established. Officials identified it as fragments of an aerial drone, not a maritime drone. How the fragments relate to each other, how many separate drones they came from and their exact provenance were under investigation, and no flight route was observed.

Military divers and explosive-ordnance disposal specialists, the Coast Guard and other emergency services responded and recovered the material. No pyrotechnic risk was reported for the material examined, and no injuries were reported. Appeals prosecutors in Constanța were notified.
