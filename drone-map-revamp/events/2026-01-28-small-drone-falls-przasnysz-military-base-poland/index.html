Crash · Poland

Small drone of unknown origin falls on Przasnysz military base in Poland

28 January 2026 · Przasnysz

Unresolved origin

On 28 January 2026 security staff at the military base in Przasnysz, Poland, saw a small, toy-like drone flying over the site before it fell. The incident became public on 2 February 2026, when it was reported that a small drone had fallen on a Polish army base.

The drone’s origin and operator are unknown, and foreign involvement has not been established. Its route before it reached the base is also unknown. It was described as an unsophisticated device, and no recording media were identified on it. An investigation was reported.

No damage or casualties were reported. The drone was secured and handed over to the Military Police.
