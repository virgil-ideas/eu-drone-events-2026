CONTSHIP JET hit by maritime drones off Cape Kaliakra, minister says

28 October 2026 · CONTSHIP JET off Cape Kaliakra · Reported maritime-drone strike

Drone strike confirmed in ministerial statement; all 18 crew safe according to ship manager; final vessel condition unverified

CONTSHIP JET was reported attacked off Bulgaria’s Cape Kaliakra on 28 October while sailing from Haifa toward Constanța. Bulgaria’s defence minister said three maritime drones struck a container ship during that day’s trilateral meeting. Preliminary maritime reporting places the attack outside Bulgarian territorial waters.

The crew reportedly abandoned ship in life rafts, and the ship manager later said all 18 were safe. Listing and lost containers were reported; final vessel condition, drone model and operator remain unresolved. This is one offshore shipping occurrence, not three events or a confirmed Bulgarian territorial incursion.
