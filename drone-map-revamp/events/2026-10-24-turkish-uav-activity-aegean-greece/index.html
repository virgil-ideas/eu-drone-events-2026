Greece reports 7 Turkish UAVs and 6 airspace violations on 24 October

24 October 2026 · Northeastern / central / southeastern Aegean · Greek-reported Turkish UAVs

Greek-authority-reported Turkish UAV airspace violations

The Greek military account for 24 October 2026 reports 7 Turkish UAVs, 6 national-airspace violations and 11 Athens FIR infringements across the northeastern, central and southeastern Aegean. This is one daily-activity record, not a sum of the three measures.

Flight and Veteranos publish matching accounts attributed to GEETHA. The original bulletin was not directly retrieved, and the publications are not independent flight observations. Identification and interception were reported, with no armed engagement reported; precise tracks, times and payloads remain unknown.
