Brief drone incursion near Chilia on 2 May

2 May 2026 · Chilia · 2026-05-02

MApN-confirmed brief drone incursion, reported by HotNews.ro

MApN reported a brief radar-confirmed entry near Chilia, followed by movement toward Ismail. Two F-16s launched around 02:00; the northern Tulcea warning ran from 02:03 to 02:50 local.

The entry is recorded separately from the wider attack on Ukraine. Intent and the specific target remain unestablished; no Romanian impact is reported.
