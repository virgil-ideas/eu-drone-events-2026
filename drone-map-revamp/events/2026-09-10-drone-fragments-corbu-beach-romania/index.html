Drone fragments of unknown origin reported on Corbu beach, Romania

10 September 2026 · Corbu — beach

Reported aerial-drone-fragment discovery; foreign origin unresolved

Police were notified at about 09:40 local time on 10 September 2026 of drone fragments on Corbu beach in Constanța County, on Romania's Black Sea coast. This is the notification time, not a verified crash time.

The cited accounts do not establish an operator or origin, and it is not known how many aircraft the fragments came from. Finding the fragments does not prove a same-day incursion into Romanian airspace, and the route is unknown.

No explosive material was reported after the fragments were examined, and they were handed to the Coast Guard or other competent authorities. The Corbu notification is distinct from the 08:30 report of fragments at Mamaia Nord that morning and from the Capu Midia discovery the evening before.
