Suspected drone fragment of unknown origin found at Agigea, Romania

26 September 2026 · Agigea · reported drone fragment

Reported drone-fragment discovery; identification and foreign origin unresolved

On the morning of 26 September 2026 fishermen found a suspected drone fragment among the coastal blocks at Agigea, in Constanța County, Romania, and notified the authorities. Antena 3 CNN described a pale, wing-like object, possibly part of an aerial drone. The date is that of the discovery; when and how the object reached the shore is unknown.

Radio România reported an ongoing investigation. Whether the object is a drone, its model, its origin, its operator and when it was lost have not been established. The discovery alone does not show that a drone crossed into Romania, and it has not been linked to any tracked incursion.

Preliminary reporting showed no attached explosive load, but no final official pyrotechnic assessment had been published. No casualties, damage or detonation were reported, and the reports do not establish that the fragment was physically retrieved.
