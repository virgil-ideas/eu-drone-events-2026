UAV fragments recovered at Popski Beach; origin unresolved

21 October 2026 · Popski Beach, Tsarevo · Unidentified UAV fragments

MoD-attributed UAV-part recovery; national origin and flight/crash date unresolved

OFFNews quotes the Bulgarian defence ministry confirming that a Navy team examined and collected UAV parts at Popski Beach on 21 October and transported them to base. This dates a recovery operation, not a flight or crash.

The ministry explicitly distinguished the recovery from an unverified nearby Arapya allegation. Model, origin, operator, payload and arrival mechanism remain unresolved. This provisional recovery candidate is not a confirmed foreign incursion or evidence of another distinct airframe.
