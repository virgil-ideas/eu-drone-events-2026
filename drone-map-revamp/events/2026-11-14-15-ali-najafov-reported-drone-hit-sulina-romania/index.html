ALI NAJAFOV reports overnight drone hit near Danube mouths

14 November 2026 – 15 November 2026 · ALI NAJAFOV · reported drone hit near the Danube mouths

Reported maritime drone impact, relayed by Romanian authorities

ALI NAJAFOV reported a drone hit during the night of 14–15 November near the Danube mouths. Romanian authorities relayed the vessel report; the crew extinguished a fire and no casualties were reported.

This is a separate vessel from EVIA PETROL V and MARSOL. Its exact location and maritime jurisdiction remain unresolved; no common operator or deliberate targeting is established.
