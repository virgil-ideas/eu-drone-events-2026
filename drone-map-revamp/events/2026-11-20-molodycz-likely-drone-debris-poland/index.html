Mołodycz: prosecutors preliminarily identify cruise-missile debris

20 November 2026 · Mołodycz · preliminarily identified cruise-missile debris

Airspace breach confirmed; preliminary missile-fragment identification; excluded from active drone totals

Excluded from the map and totals. Lublin prosecutors’ preliminary 22 November examination identified Kh-101 cruise-missile fragments at Mołodycz, superseding the earlier drone suspicion. Expert examination is pending; current evidence does not support counting this as a drone event. This page preserves the correction and its sources.

Poland confirmed that three objects entered its airspace on 20 November. Poland’s National Security Bureau initially assessed the debris as likely from a jet-powered drone. The separate airspace breach is a confirmed occurrence, not proof that a drone was among the objects.

On 22 November, the Lublin District Prosecutor’s Office said preliminary specialist examinations found Kh-101 cruise-missile fragments at Nowy Lipowiec, Rudnik nad Sanem and Mołodycz. Mołodycz scene examination ended on 23 November. The site was cleared, checked for safety and handed back on 24 November. On 24 November the prosecutor’s office said that crews had left all three sites and that laboratory and expert analysis was underway.

This page keeps the original date and URL to document the correction. The record is excluded from active drone totals because the latest evidence does not support the earlier drone assessment. The intended target and whether the border crossing was deliberate remain unestablished.

The Lublin prosecutor’s 23 November written summary describes one investigation across all three sites, with laboratory and expert work pending. Poland’s NATO delegation says the emergency meeting that day concerned the 20 November missile breach, not the separate drone entry on 23 November.
