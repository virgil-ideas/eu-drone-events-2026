Recovery · Lithuania

Old drone found in forest at Keneliai, Pasvalys district, Lithuania

16 September 2026 · Keneliai — Pasvalys district forest

Drone recovery; foreign origin unproven; later smuggling-investigation link

On 16 September 2026 a drone that appeared to have been lying in vegetation for months was found in a forest at Keneliai village, in Lithuania’s Pasvalys district, and reported to police. The date is that of the discovery and report, not evidence of a flight that day.

Initial speculation that it was a foreign military drone was not confirmed by the later police account. The drone was not identified as a Russian or Ukrainian strike drone, and police later associated it with a smuggling investigation. Its foreign origin has not been publicly established, and how it reached the forest is not known.

ARAS and the military responded to the possible explosive risk. In an update on 17 September, police said no explosives had been found.
