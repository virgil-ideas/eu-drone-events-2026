Unidentified target enters Romanian airspace near Chilia Veche

16 April 2026 – 17 April 2026 · Chilia Veche · overnight unidentified target entry

MApN-confirmed aerial-target entry; drone identity unconfirmed

MApN reported one national-airspace entry during the night of 16–17 April. Contact was lost around 16 km southeast of Chilia Veche, and a search mission was announced.

The official release describes an aerial target, not an identified drone. Neither radar-contact loss nor a planned search establishes a crash or debris recovery.
