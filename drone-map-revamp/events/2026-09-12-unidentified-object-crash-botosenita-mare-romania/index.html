12 September 2026 · Botoșenița Mare · unidentified flying object crash

MApN-reported crashed object; identity linkage unresolved

MApN reported a crashed flying object in a cornfield near Botoșenița Mare on 12 September, with a small fire and no casualties.

Foreign authorities also reported drone transit and a 112 sighting was logged elsewhere. A continuous link to the recovered object is not established.
