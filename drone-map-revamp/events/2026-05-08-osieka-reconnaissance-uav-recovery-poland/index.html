UAV recovered near Osieka after prosecutor-reported crossing from Russia

8 May 2026 · Osieka · reconnaissance UAV recovery

Officially reported foreign incursion and recovery; operator unresolved

An unarmed reconnaissance UAV was recovered whole after a civilian field discovery near Osieka on 9 May. On 11 May the Olsztyn prosecutor spokesman said investigators were certain it had crossed the Russian–Polish border and dated the crossing under investigation to 8 May.

The evidential basis for that crossing finding was not disclosed. Operator, manufacture, intended target, precise track and crash time remain unresolved. Media claims of cameras and Cyrillic markings were not confirmed by the prosecutor. This is one retrospective occurrence, not new October activity.
