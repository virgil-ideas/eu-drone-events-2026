LARISSA 1 damaged in reported drone hit off Romania; crew safe

13 November 2026 · LARISSA 1 off Sfântu Gheorghe · Reported drone hit

Drone hit acknowledged by defence minister; vessel and territorial-water position identified in reporting

A reported aerial-drone hit damaged the cargo crane of LARISSA 1 near Sfântu Gheorghe on the evening of 13 November 2026. Maritime authorities were notified around 21:40 local time. No injuries, deaths or fire were reported.

Romania’s defence minister acknowledged the overnight hit on 14 November. TVR identifies the vessel, while Europa FM places the reported incident 6.8 nautical miles northeast of Sfântu Gheorghe in Romanian territorial waters. Exact impact coordinates have not been published.

The operator, origin and intended target remain unknown. Technical findings were pending, and no link to the separate Tulcea aerial-target alert has been established.
