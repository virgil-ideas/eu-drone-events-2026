Drone strike sets WINE TRADER on fire off Sulina; seven crew missing

15 October 2026 · WINE TRADER off Sulina · Drone strike and fire

Drone strike confirmed by Romania’s president; Russian origin assessed by a NATO military source; five rescued and seven missing

A drone struck the San Marino-flagged tanker WINE TRADER off Sulina on 15 October 2026. Romania’s president confirmed the attack. A NATO military source cited by Reuters assessed the drones that hit this tanker and YARA J as Russian in origin; a specific operator has not been established in the reviewed reporting.

Of twelve crew, five were rescued and seven remained missing. Two survivors had scalp burns or mild smoke inhalation; all five were stable and taken to hospital. Fire damaged the vessel. Its final condition is unconfirmed, and the missing crew are not counted as confirmed dead. Heavy seas hindered the continuing rescue operation. IGSU reported that the search resumed on 16 October; announced boarding plans are not confirmation of completed boarding.

The ship was reported about 13 nautical miles southeast of Sulina, just outside Romanian territorial waters. It is included in the register’s offshore coverage. The rescue request at 06:58 is not a verified attack time. This promotes the earlier fire-only watchlist lead to one red Strike record, combining the attack, fire and rescue; it remains separate from YARA J and Sea Destiny.

MRCC-coordinated rescue. SAR Apollo departed at 07:10 after the 06:58 request; helicopters and rescue vessels were deployed. Five crew were evacuated by helicopter, and search preparations continued for the seven missing. Heavy seas complicated operations and forced a smaller Coast Guard craft to withdraw. IGSU said search-and-rescue operations resumed on the morning of 16 October. Its announced plans included a Black Hawk transporting four rescuers aboard WINE TRADER and FIFI MAI 0101 participating with thirteen rescuers.
