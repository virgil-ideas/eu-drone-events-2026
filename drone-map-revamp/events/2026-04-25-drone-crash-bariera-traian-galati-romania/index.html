Crash · Romania

Drone reported as Russian crashes in Galați, Romania, damaging property

25 April 2026 · Bariera Traian, Galați

Reported foreign

On 25 April 2026 a military drone crashed in the Bariera Traian area of Galați, Romania. It reportedly approached at low altitude over Lake Brateș, although its precise track has not been independently reconstructed. The drone damaged an electricity pole and a household or workshop structure.

Contemporaneous coverage of the explosive disposal reported the drone as Russian. Its model was not specified. RAF Typhoon fighters had been scrambled for threats nearby, but this drone was not shot down.

Explosive material was confirmed in the drone, and the recovered explosives were detonated in a controlled disposal the same day. The crash caused property damage, but no casualties were reported. It is a separate incident from a drone impact on an apartment building in Galați on 29 May 2026.
