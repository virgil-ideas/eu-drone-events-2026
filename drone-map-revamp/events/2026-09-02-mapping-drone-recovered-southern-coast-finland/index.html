Pellinki drone found on 1 September; likely Russian origin assessed

1 September 2026 · Pellinki · Drone discovery and preliminary Russian-origin assessment

Recovered UAV; likely Russia-origin assessment, investigation ongoing

A civilian found a UAV among Pellinki’s shore rocks on 1 September 2026; a Border Guard patrol confirmed it. The earlier register date, 2 September, was the initial release date. The published page address is preserved.

On 10 September the Border Guard said device data suggested a likely Russia-origin aircraft that lost control and ran out of fuel before falling into the sea outside Finland. The finding was preliminary and the investigation continued.

This remains one recovery, not an established aerial incursion or crash in Finland. Operator and original purpose remain unresolved. The map point is an approximate archipelago anchor.
