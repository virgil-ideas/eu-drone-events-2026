Recovery · Finland

Mapping drone of unknown origin recovered on Finland's southern coast

2 September 2026 · Southern Finnish coast

Recovery; foreign status unresolved

On 2 September 2026 authorities recovered a survey or mapping drone (UAV) on the southern coast of Finland. The precise location was not given in the cited report. The date is the recovery date; when the drone flew, and along which route, is unknown.

The drone's origin and operator are unknown. It has not been established that it came from abroad, and it is not assumed to be a foreign military drone.

The drone was described as non-dangerous, with no hazardous payload identified, and no damage or casualties were reported. Authorities investigated whether the flight amounted to a territorial violation.
