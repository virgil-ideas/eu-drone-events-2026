Recovery · Poland

Drone recovered at Rusinowo beach; warhead disposed of safely

14 September 2026 · Rusinowo · Jarosławiec coast

Military-drone recovery; preliminary Russian-made Gerbera identification

A naval crew observed a UAV drifting near Jarosławiec around 09:20 on 14 September; it washed ashore near Rusinowo around 11:25, according to the Poznań prosecutor’s 1 October statement. This is one recovery and disposal occurrence, not another event on the follow-up publication date.

Specialists separated a warhead with a potential explosive charge and destroyed it in a controlled detonation. Investigators preliminarily identified a Russian-made Gerbera. The operator, target, flight route and sea-impact circumstances remain unresolved; manufacture does not establish operator or intent.

The investigation transferred to Poznań on 25 September and specialist examinations were ordered. No casualties or damage were reported.
