Tausiūnai–Aukštalaukis: border guards recover drone carrying cigarettes from Belarus

27 September 2026 · Tausiūnai–Aukštalaukis · cross-border smuggling drone

Officially reported cross-border smuggling-drone incident

VSAT reported a cigarette-carrying drone from Belarus late Sunday evening, local time, on 27 September. The drone was detected near Tausiūnai and recovered near Aukštalaukis after a forced descent. It was badly damaged.

Border guards recovered 250 packs of cigarettes and opened administrative proceedings. The operator was not identified. This is a cross-border smuggling case, not an established state attack.
