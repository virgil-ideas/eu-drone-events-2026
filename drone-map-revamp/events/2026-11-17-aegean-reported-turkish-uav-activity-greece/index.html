Aegean · five UAVs in Greek daily report for 17 November

17 November 2026 · Aegean · 17 November reported Turkish UAV activity

Reported Greek-authority daily figures for Turkish UAV airspace violations

Greek reporting reproduces GEETHA’s 17 November table listing five Turkish UAVs, four national-airspace violations and seven Athens FIR traffic-rule infringements across the Aegean.

This is one daily regional record; aircraft and crossing figures are non-additive. The fighter encounter in the same bulletin does not establish a drone shoot-down. Exact tracks and intended targets remain unknown; the primary archive gap and Greek-Turkish airspace dispute remain relevant.
