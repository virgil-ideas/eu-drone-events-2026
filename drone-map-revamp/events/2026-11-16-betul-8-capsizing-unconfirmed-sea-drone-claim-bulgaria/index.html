BETUL 8 capsizes off Bulgaria; crew member alleges sea-drone attack

16 November 2026 · BETUL 8 · capsizing with unconfirmed sea-drone claim

Capsizing and rescue confirmed; sea-drone attack alleged by a crew member

Bulgarian authorities confirmed that BETUL 8 capsized in the country’s exclusive economic zone on 16 November. All 12 crew were evacuated; the ministry reported no serious injuries.

BNR quoted one rescued sailor alleging an attack by two sea drones. Authorities had not confirmed that cause in the statements reviewed. The offshore casualty is retained as a candidate, not a confirmed drone strike or territorial incursion.

Bulgarian NAVWARN 860/26 gives a later observation at 14:45 UTC on 19 November: the vessel was capsized and drifting without crew or control. This is not a final-condition or cause determination.
