Explosion · Lithuania

Drone explosion near Juodžionys damages a passing car

18 October 2026 · Juodžionys · drone explosion and debris

Foreign drone incursion and reported explosion; Ukrainian origin identified by Lithuanian and Polish officials

A drone entered Lithuania on the morning of 18 October 2026 and reportedly exploded near Juodžionys in Pasvalys district. Debris damaged a passing car without injuring its occupants. The precise sequence of the explosion and fall remained under investigation.

Defence Minister Robertas Kaunas linked the craft to Ukraine’s attack on St Petersburg and assessed that Russian electronic warfare had diverted it. On 19 October, Lithuania’s prime minister said wreckage and Ukraine’s notification supported Ukrainian origin and that the drone carried explosives. Poland’s defence ministry also identified it as Ukrainian and denied the claim that it had launched from Lithuania. The jamming explanation remained an assessment.

Lithuania’s published chronology puts first radar contact at 05:47 EEST over Poland and entry into Lithuania at 06:02. Polish radar did not independently corroborate that track. NATO fighters scrambled but did not shoot. The initial Latvian NBS account did not indicate entry into Latvia by this flight; unverified Polish reports are not additional confirmed occurrences.

On 20 October, President Nausėda said the Lithuanian and Polish defence ministries had formed a joint investigation group. The full route and crash mechanism remained unresolved.
