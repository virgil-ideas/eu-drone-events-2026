Explosion · Lithuania

Drone explosion near Juodžionys damages a passing car

18 October 2026 · Juodžionys · drone explosion and debris

Reported foreign incursion and explosion; preliminary Ukrainian attribution

A drone entered Lithuania on the morning of 18 October 2026 and reportedly exploded near Juodžionys in Pasvalys district. Debris damaged a passing car without injuring its occupants. The precise sequence of the explosion and fall remained under investigation.

Defence Minister Robertas Kaunas later linked the craft to Ukraine’s attack on St Petersburg and assessed that Russian electronic warfare had diverted it. This was a preliminary account. NATO fighters scrambled but did not shoot; the Latvian warning and unverified Polish reports are not additional confirmed incursions.
