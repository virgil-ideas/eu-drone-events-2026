KHORYV drone-strike report: vessel capsized but still afloat

23 October 2026 · KHORYV off Constanța · Reported drone strike, fire and capsize

Reported drone strike; capsized and still afloat per navigation notice; one dead and three missing

KHORYV capsized outside Romanian territorial waters on 23 October. AGERPRES links it to the president’s statement that a drone struck a vessel. The current primary navigation notice, ARN158, reports KHORYV partially submerged but still afloat upside down as of 27 October, despite press reports of sinking. Completed sinking is not treated as established.

Romania reports thirteen crew: nine rescued, one dead and three missing. Ukraine confirms a Ukrainian fatality but reports fourteen crew; the discrepancy remains unresolved. The notices give dated capsize and subsequent drifting-wreck positions, not a verified strike coordinate. Operator attribution and the final search outcome remain unresolved.

Bulgaria announced monitoring and a planned naval inspection on 27 October. The next morning its defence minister said the wreck was entering Bulgarian territorial sea. These are later drifting-wreck reports, not a new attack or a change to the original offshore strike location.

Damage / casualties / disruption
Romanian authorities reported thirteen crew: nine rescued, one dead and three missing. Some reporting describes sinking, but the current navigation notice says it remains afloat inverted. Ukraine’s MFA confirmed a Ukrainian fatality and four Ukrainians hospitalized, including the captain; two others did not require medical care. Its fourteen-crew figure conflicts with Romania’s thirteen; no reconciled manifest or final missing-person outcome was retrieved.
Response / outcome
A nearby merchant vessel recovered survivors before transfer to Romanian rescue services. MRCC coordinated ARSVOM and Interior Ministry assets. Weather prevented helicopter assistance; nine survivors subsequently reached Constanța for assessment. ARSVOM, as reported on 24 October, deployed SAR ARTEMIS at 14:24; MRCC suspended the search at 18:04 because of reduced visibility. This was not a final missing-person determination. ARN158 retains three missing and a recommended one-nautical-mile safety distance as of 27 October. Bulgaria’s transport ministry said on 27 October that institutions were monitoring the drifting wreck and would send a naval vessel for inspection. On 28 October BGNES quoted the defence minister saying it was entering Bulgarian territorial sea and that naval and transport authorities were ready to respond.
