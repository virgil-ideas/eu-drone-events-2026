Flight · Romania

Reported Russian jet drone crosses into northeastern Romania from Moldova

8 September 2026 · Neamț · Botoșani · Iași sector

Reported foreign

On 8 September 2026 a jet-powered drone entered Romanian airspace from Moldova at about 16:37 local time and crossed the Neamț, Botoșani and Iași sector of northeastern Romania. It stayed in Romanian airspace for roughly 30 minutes before leaving for Moldova, where it reportedly crashed. Timings for individual parts of the flight vary between accounts.

The drone was reported as Russian. Its technical identification as a jet-powered Geran-4 is attributed to Romanian and Moldovan reporting.

Two Spanish F/A-18 fighters were scrambled, and authorities monitored the flight and warned residents. Restrictions were imposed at Iași Airport and civil alerts were issued. No impact or casualties were reported in Romania; the reported crash happened outside the EU, in Moldova.
