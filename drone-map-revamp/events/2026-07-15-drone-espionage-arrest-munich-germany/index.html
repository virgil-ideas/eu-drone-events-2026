Germany arrests Moldovan over suspected drone espionage in Munich

15 July 2026 · Munich — defence-manufacturer site

Arrest and espionage allegation; foreign sponsor unspecified

On the evening of Wednesday 15 July 2026, residents in Munich, Germany, alerted police to a drone filming the premises of a weapons manufacturer. A Reuters security source named the company as KNDS. A 37-year-old Moldovan suspect was arrested near the site, and an arrest warrant and remand were announced on Friday 17 July.

Investigators alleged that he was gathering intelligence for a foreign entity or prohibited organisation, but no sponsoring state has been named. The drone is alleged to have been launched locally; no cross-border flight is alleged. The suspect's citizenship alone does not show direction by a foreign state, and the case is an allegation, not a conviction.

The drone's model was not specified, and no payload or damage was reported.
