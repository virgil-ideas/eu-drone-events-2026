Aegean · seven UAVs in Greek daily report for 16 November

16 November 2026 · Aegean · 16 November reported Turkish UAV activity

Reported Greek-authority daily figures for Turkish UAV airspace violations

Parapolitika reproduced GEETHA’s 16 November daily table reporting seven Turkish UAVs, six national-airspace violations and nine Athens FIR traffic-rule infringements in the northeastern, central and southeastern Aegean.

The account reports identification and routine interception, with no engagements. This is one daily regional record, not six separate incidents or fifteen incursions. Exact tracks and intended targets remain unknown; the Greek-Turkish airspace dispute remains relevant.
