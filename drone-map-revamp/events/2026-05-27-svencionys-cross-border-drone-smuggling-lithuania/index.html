Lithuanian prosecutors document May drone-smuggling operation in Švenčionys district

27 May 2026 · Švenčionys district · drone-smuggling operation

Cross-border drone smuggling documented by Lithuanian prosecutors

Lithuanian prosecutors said drones brought 35,500 cigarette packs across the border from Belarus on the night of 27 May. The aircraft count is unknown.

Guards recovered cigarettes after they were moved on the ground. No drone interception or recovery was reported. The court order was not final at the time of the 20 November announcement.
