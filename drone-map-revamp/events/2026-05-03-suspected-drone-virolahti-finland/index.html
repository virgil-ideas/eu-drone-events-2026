Suspected drone incursion reported at Virolahti in southeastern Finland

3 May 2026 · Virolahti

Suspected foreign incursion

On 3 May 2026 a drone track was reported in the border airspace at Virolahti in southeastern Finland. The object, described as a suspected drone, was reported to have left in the direction of Russia.

The object’s origin and model have not been resolved, and its attribution remains open. The case was treated as a suspected violation of Finnish territory rather than a confirmed one.

No impact was reported. The track was monitored and the incident was investigated.
