Lithuanian guards report bringing down four smuggling drones near Jaskonys

3 July 2026 · Jaskonys · four cross-border smuggling drones

Officially reported cross-border smuggling-drone operation

Lithuanian border guards reported bringing down four inbound drones in one operation near Jaskonys, recovering the aircraft and 4,998 cigarette packs.

The 7 July release refers to Friday night, with recoveries continuing into daylight. It does not specify the midnight boundary. This is not the later Kuodžiai operation.
