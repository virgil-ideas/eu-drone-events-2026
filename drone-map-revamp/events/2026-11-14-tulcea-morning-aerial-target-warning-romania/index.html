Tulcea receives two warnings during one morning aerial-target alert

14 November 2026 · Eastern and northern Tulcea · 14 November morning warning

Officially reported aerial-target alert; drone identity and Romanian entry not established

MApN reported an aerial target in the Snake Island area on the morning of 14 November 2026. Two Romanian F-16s launched from Fetești.

Eastern Tulcea received a warning at 04:45 local, followed by northern Tulcea at 05:39. The all-clear came at 05:52. These messages belong to one alert episode, not two confirmed drone occurrences.

The statement did not identify the target as a drone or confirm Romanian entry. This selected alert is separate from the previous evening’s warning; no connection to the Larissa 1 hit is established.
