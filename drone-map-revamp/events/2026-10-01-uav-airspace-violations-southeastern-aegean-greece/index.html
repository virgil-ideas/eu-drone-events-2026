Greece reports two airspace violations by a Turkish UAV in the Aegean

1 October 2026 · Southeastern Aegean · Greek-reported Turkish UAV

Greek-authority-reported Turkish UAV airspace violations

On 1 October 2026 Greece’s Hellenic National Defence General Staff reported that one Turkish UAV had made two violations of national airspace and one infringement of air-traffic rules in the Athens flight information region (FIR) over the southeastern Aegean. OnAlert relayed the account that evening, followed by NewsIT. No island, coordinates, times or flight track were published.

The Turkish identification and the violation assessment are those of the Greek military, and the flight track has not been independently verified. The drone’s model was not specified. The three figures are different measures of one drone’s activity, not separate aircraft or incidents.

Greek forces reportedly identified and intercepted the UAV. No weapons engagement, shoot-down, crash, casualties or damage were reported.
