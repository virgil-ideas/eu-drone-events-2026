Explosion · Romania

Fatal drone attack reported on grain ship Royad Mammadov off Romania

5 October 2026 · Royad Mammadov · reported drone attack

Fatal drone attack reported; ship later reported afloat; casualty accounts conflict

On 5 October 2026 the grain carrier Royad Mammadov, sailing from Izmail in Ukraine to Ravenna in Italy, was damaged by a reported drone strike, an onboard blast and fire in the Black Sea near the Pescăruș platform, about 30 nautical miles south of Sfântu Gheorghe. The site was in Romania’s exclusive economic zone (EEZ), outside its territorial waters. A rescue request was logged at 10:37 local time; the exact strike time is not verified.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said two Russian drones struck the ship. That is a Ukrainian claim, not independent verification, and Romania has published no attribution finding. Marius Roibu, director of the MRCC Constanța rescue centre, described an apparent multiple-drone attack; the drones’ type, launch point and operators are unestablished.

Casualty accounts conflict. Romania’s Interior Ministry initially reported two dead and eleven rescued; on 6 October Azerbaijan’s Foreign Ministry said the captain had been killed and another Azerbaijani crew member was missing. India’s embassy said three Indian crew members survived, one of them injured. An initial report that the ship sank was corrected on 6 October, when it was reported afloat and awaiting a tow to Midia. Petrom said the Pescăruș platform was unaffected.
