Drone of unknown origin found near Șerbăuți in Suceava County, Romania

3 May 2026 · Șerbăuți

Recovery; origin unresolved

On 3 May 2026 a drone was found in a rural area near Șerbăuți in Suceava County, Romania, around 20 km from Ukraine, near Romania’s northern border. The date is when the drone was found and reported. When it actually came down, and the route it took to get there, are unknown.

The available accounts do not establish the drone’s nationality or its operator, and its model was not specified. Its identity and provenance remain unresolved.

No harm was reported. Romanian authorities investigated the find, and the drone was recovered.
